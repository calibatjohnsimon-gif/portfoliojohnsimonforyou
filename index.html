About

My name is John Simon Calibat. I have been working as a freelancer Graphic designer, Video editor, and UX/UI Designer for over 3 years, specializing in branding, Posters, logo, Modern Design, and illustration. I have collaborated with clients from around the world, and every project starts with a clear strategy and trust.

A design is more than just aesthetics. It is a powerful tool for communication, so I spend as much time on the concept as I do on the pixels. My goal is to create work that stands the test of time.
